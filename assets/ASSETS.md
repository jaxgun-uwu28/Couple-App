# Phase 0 assets

No production sprites, maps, fonts or audio have been imported. Phaser's hello-world canvas draws shapes and system-font text. The approved generated reference boards in `docs/mockups` are concept references only, with their prompts and limitations recorded there.

The generated Capacitor Android launcher/splash assets are temporary tool defaults from the MIT-licensed Capacitor package, not a new approved Paw & Us icon. Replace them only under the relevant design gate. Phaser 3 is MIT licensed; React and Vite are MIT licensed. Dependency licenses remain in their packages.
